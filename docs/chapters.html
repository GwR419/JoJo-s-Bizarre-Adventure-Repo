- Before Viewing -

  • Something to note for manga reading. Manga books are printed and read different then here in the United States.
  • As manga author are orgianlly written in Japan. They are read from back to front, right to left, as this is how manga are printed.
  • Although from our website it is still coded to the english verison it will still be coded if you read it in japanese it will be right left.
  • Manga reads right-to-left (and top-to-bottom within columns)because it follows traditional Japanese writing, where text flows from right to left, unlike Western left-to-right reading.
  • To preserve the original art, panel flow, and creator's intent, publishers keep this original format, meaning you start at
  • the back cover and read pages backward, with panels and speech bubbles also ordered right-to-left on each page.
