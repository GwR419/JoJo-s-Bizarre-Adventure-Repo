About Our Project

For our project, we looked at the manga series JoJo's Bizzare Adventure: Golden Wind. This is the central content area. We did in total 9 chapters, converting each into TEI using CBML. This let us convert the TEI/XML into HTML using XSLT. It will scroll "over" the fixed background image.
