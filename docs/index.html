About Our Project

For our project, we looked at the manga series JoJo's Bizzare Adventure: Golden Wind. We did in total 9 chapters, converting each into TEI using CBML. This let us visualize our code in many ways, converting it into HTML using XSLT.

What We Want People to Take Away?

Our goal for this project was we wanted to share with people how the author of JOJO's Bizarre Adventures, Hirohiko Araki, writes and bolds specific text/words in his manga. As Golden Wind is one of the most popular of the PARTS of JOJO's, we thought it would be a great one to do our work on.
